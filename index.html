Advancing Science, Building Partnerships, Empowering Minds

Norfolk State University proudly announces the Grand Opening of:

The Center for Molecular Diagnostic Testing and
Next-Generation Sequencing

This state-of-the-art facility will:

  • Combine cutting-edge next-generation sequencing technology with academic expertise.
  • Advance critical biomedical research in pharmacogenetics and pharmacogenomics.
  • Address unmet healthcare needs in underrepresented minority communities.
  • Foster partnerships with healthcare providers, research institutions, and industry leaders.
  • Prepare the next generation of scientists and healthcare professionals.

“We welcome you to join us in celebrating this momentous occasion and look forward to meeting you at the event.”

Joseph C. Hall, Ph.D.
Professor of Chemistry and Center Director

“This new center represents a major step forward for our university and for the broader community. By combining cutting-edge technology with a commitment to health equity, we believe this center will drive meaningful change and make a lasting impact on public health.”

Javaune Adams-Gaston, Ph.D.
President, Norfolk State University
